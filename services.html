02 / SELF-MANAGED PREP

What you organise for the Standard Showreel

Come prepared
to film.

The Standard Showreel is for dancers who want Temujin to film and edit at a professional level, while they manage the creative preparation before the shoot.

01

Choose the music

Select one track that can carry the full reel. The edit is built around one strong piece of music, not disconnected clips.

02

Prepare five to six styles

Bring choreography that shows range, control and personality across the styles you want casting teams or employers to see.

03

Plan the strongest opening

Your best material should appear early. Temujin can advise on flow, but the dancer needs to arrive knowing what they want to show.

04

Think about transitions

The first and last counts of each section matter. Matching energy, shape or timing between styles helps the final edit feel intentional.

05

Handle styling and support

Hair, makeup, outfits, choreography support and studio booking are organised by the dancer unless a guided package is chosen.

06

Practise before shoot day

The shoot moves faster when the material is clean, remembered and ready. The goal is to spend the session capturing strong takes, not solving the routine.

04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Clear before
you book.

What should I choose?

If you are a dancer and need a reel for auditions, start with the showreel packages. For classes, events, music videos, self-tests or audio recording, send the project details and Temujin will quote the right setup.

What costs extra?

Studio hire, location hire, travel, extra crew, rushed delivery or third-party services are confirmed before booking.

Can I enquire before I’m ready?

Yes. Temujin can help you work out what needs to be prepared before filming, especially if you are unsure about music, styles, choreography or transitions.
