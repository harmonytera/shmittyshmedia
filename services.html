What if I don’t know what I need?

Tell Temujin what opportunity you are pursuing. He will recommend the most suitable type of media and level of support.

Are prices fixed?

Package prices and inclusions will be editable as the business develops. Studio hire, travel and third-party services will be explained clearly before booking.

Can other performers enquire?

Yes. Showreels are the primary service, but Shmitty ShMEDIA works with performers and creatives across a range of projects.
