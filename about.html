A performer’s perspective

He knows what
needs to be seen.

Temujin’s professional career has taken him through theatre, cruise-ship entertainment and international performance. He has been in the room as the person auditioning, preparing and trying to make a strong impression.

That experience shapes the way he creates media. He does not expect performers to arrive knowing exactly what to make. He helps them understand what their reel needs to communicate, then builds the project around the opportunity they want.

02 / THE ROYAL CARIBBEAN STORY

A real client outcome

From being cut
to being hired.

One dancer had auditioned for cruise contracts three years in a row and kept being cut in the live audition process. After Temujin created a professional showreel for her, she was hired by Royal Caribbean.

The reel did not guarantee the result. It gave her a stronger way to present her ability before the next opportunity.
